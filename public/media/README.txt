Luxury hero video slot
-----------------------
Place the final luxury hero MP4 at:
  public/media/luxury-hero.mp4

The hero tries this local file first, then falls back to the configured/remote film.
Keep the video landscape (16:9-ish), muted-autoplay friendly, and encoded as H.264/AAC.
